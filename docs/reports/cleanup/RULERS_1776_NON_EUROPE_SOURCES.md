# RULERS 1776 — NON-EUROPE SOURCES

## Scope

This research packet covers exactly the 140 centralized non-European tags left in the CLEANUP-2C-0 historical-research queue: 2 P0 cases, 135 P1 generated-ruler cases, and 3 P2 scripted-but-unverified cases. The 8 already validated Batch-1 countries and all 165 decentralized tags are outside this packet.

Start-date rule: every historical conclusion is evaluated for **1776-01-01**, not merely for the calendar year 1776.

## Source policy

- No exact date is invented. When only a year or approximate chronology is supportable, the MASTER records an approximate/unknown date policy.
- A governor, company director, bey, pasha, regent, chief, almamy, ataliq or other delegated executive is not promoted into a sovereign monarch.
- A collective or fragmented polity is not assigned a fictional monarch merely because Victoria 3 prefers a visible ruler.
- When the fork tag itself is anachronistic or geographically over-broad, the result is explicitly deferred to map/political-structure work.
- `HIGH`, `MEDIUM`, and `LOW` confidence are evidence-quality flags, not gameplay priority.
- URLs in this report are research locators. Some are reference/encyclopedic gateways used to corroborate ruler sequences; the MASTER notes low confidence wherever the surviving accessible chronology is weak or conflicting.

## P0 / P2 critical corrections

### DEI
Recommended start-date executive: **Jeremias van Riemsdijk**, Governor-General of the Dutch East Indies. He entered office on 1775-12-28. Reinier de Klerk is a later successor and must not remain the 1776-01-01 ruler. The office is a VOC delegated executive, not a sovereign monarchy.

### USA
There was no office of President of the United States on 1776-01-01. **George Washington belongs in the military-command sphere**, while the Second Continental Congress was the political organ and **John Hancock** its presiding president. The temporary character-layer abstraction may therefore use Hancock as `President of the Continental Congress`, while the tag/map/subject contradiction remains deferred.

### BIC
**Warren Hastings** is historically valid as the East India Company executive, but only as Governor-General of Fort William/Bengal within a delegated company structure. He is not a sovereign monarch.

### DUR
**Timur Shah Durrani** is the correct start-date Shah, having succeeded Ahmad Shah in 1772.

### IR1
The scripted `Omar Ahmad` identity is not retained. The supported officeholder is **Omar Pasha**, Pasha of Baghdad and head of the Mamluk household administration under nominal Ottoman sovereignty.
