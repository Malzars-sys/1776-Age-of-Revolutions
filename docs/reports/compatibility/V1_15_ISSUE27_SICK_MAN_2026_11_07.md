## Contract and compatibility choices

The canonical installed 1.15 `common/journal_entries/journal_entries.md:134-197` documents conclusion
`outcome` blocks, owning-country root scopes, required descriptions, and `hidden invalid_outcome` flags.
It explicitly requires **no trigger** on a timeout outcome and at most one such outcome. Current vanilla
`00_sick_man.txt:14-105` supplies the completion/timeout example. Its timeout presentation uses
`JOURNAL_ENTRY_TIMEOUT`, `JOURNAL_ENTRY_ON_TIMEOUT` and `JOURNAL_ENTRY_TIMEOUT_CONCLUSION_DESC`.
These three keys and the six standard completion/failure keys were verified in unmasked English and French
base-game localization files. No new player-facing prose is introduced.

This migration retains the **mod's** authored criteria, rather than importing unrelated vanilla revisions:

- The main journal still needs **four reform points** and retains its exact **10,950-day** timeout.
- Syria/Egypt retain the original ownership and subject checks; the new vanilla uncontested-ownership and
  subject-release conditions are not substituted.
- Economy, education, army and bureaucracy thresholds, technologies, laws and scopes remain unchanged.
- Separatism retains its strict `< 0.5` success/accumulation and `> 0.5` failure conditions, monthly pulse,
  180-point goal, event lists and DLC-dependent Eastern Crisis path.
- All eight success effects, the separatism failure effect and the main timeout effect retain every token
  and effect order, including modifiers, claims, delayed/triggered events, variables and culture scopes.
- The three Rumelian tradition removals in the timeout branch are retained, as is conditional removal of
  `outmoded_bureaucracy` in the bureaucracy success branch.
- Seven silent invalidations still test the original `sick_man_complete` condition; their original absent
  handlers remain empty effects. No new cleanup effect or historical/subgoal flag is invented.
- Modifiers while active, immediate/weekly/monthly behavior, progress bars, transfer/inheritance flags,
  weights, pinning and the relative declaration order of authored conclusion predicates are preserved.

The old and new engines' behavior when multiple predicates become true together, or at the exact timeout
boundary, has not been measured in-game. Preserving source order and predicate tokens does not certify that
engine-level precedence. Automatic removal of active modifiers and forced external journal removal also need
runtime checks. In particular, vanilla cleanup helpers absent from the mod's original handlers are not copied
blindly into the effects.
