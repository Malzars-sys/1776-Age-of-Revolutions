Render the entire fortress silhouette uncut; transparent outside the extent of its walls and ditch, no surrounding landscape, trees, river, city, sky, scenery, rectangular terrain slab, model stand, floor, baseplate or decorative background. No paper plan, divider, label, flag or other accessory. Natural muted red-brown brick, pale warm-grey stone, olive grass, brown earth and neutral slate; absolutely no blue/teal color filter. This is a painted technology icon showing the principle of Vauban fortification, not a framed building thumbnail or an exact archaeological reconstruction.
