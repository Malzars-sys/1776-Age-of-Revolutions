[h1]1776 - Age of Revolutions[/h1]

Old empires still dominate the map, but revolutions, independence movements and new political ideas are beginning to tear apart the established order.

[b]This is an unofficial fork and continuation of 1776 - Age of Revolutions.[/b] It is not an official replacement for the original mod and does not imply endorsement by its original author.

[b]Tested version:[/b] Victoria 3 1.13 / The Great Wave

[h2]About this fork[/h2]

This fork brings the original 1776 total-conversion concept forward to Victoria 3 1.13. It restores the earlier start, adapts the world and its political order to the period, and corrects accumulated incompatibilities in country setup, the map, scripts, journal entries, events, objectives, flags and the frontend.

The aim is a stable and increasingly coherent 1776 campaign, not a claim that every country or system is already complete or perfectly historical.

[h2]What is included[/h2]

[list]
[*]A 1776 start during the American Revolutionary War, with a remodeled balance of power.
[*]Period countries, subjects, borders, governments, laws, institutions and diplomatic relationships.
[*]Compatibility work for Victoria 3 1.13 / The Great Wave across legacy scripts and game APIs.
[*]Restored and corrected country-specific journal entries, events, characters and historical situations.
[*]Historical-facing flags and coats of arms for featured countries and companies.
[*]Expanded English and French objective narratives and interface text.
[*]A custom revolutionary frontend with historical artwork and music.
[/list]

[h2]Featured objectives[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]Battle for India[/b] — compete through Indian states, European companies and imperial powers for influence across the subcontinent.
[*][b]Age of Revolutions[/b] — navigate reform, privilege and revolution in France, the Thirteen Colonies and the Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth.
[*][b]Imperial Rivalries[/b] — guide Britain, Spain, Russia or Prussia through the old order's global contests.
[*][b]Mercantile Republics[/b] — preserve or transform the commercial republics of Venice and Genoa.
[*][b]Learn the Game[/b] — guided starts for Sweden, the Austrian Netherlands, Đại Nam and Denmark–Norway.
[/list]

[h2]Historical 1776 setting[/h2]

The world begins before industrialization has reshaped the international order. Colonial empires remain extensive, chartered companies wield political power, aristocratic institutions dominate much of Europe, and the American war opens a wider crisis of sovereignty. The setup has been revised for period coherence while leaving room for alternate outcomes.

[h2]Custom frontend[/h2]

The fork includes a Trafalgar loading screen, a mirrored presentation of Emanuel Leutze's [i]Washington Crossing the Delaware[/i], the [i]Age of Revolution[/i] subtitle, Doug Maxwell's [i]Cantus Firmus Monks[/i] as the looping title theme, and historical illustrations adapted to the new objectives.

[h2]Compatibility and DLC[/h2]

[b]Tested only on Victoria 3 1.13 / The Great Wave.[/b] Compatibility with later game versions is not claimed.

No DLC is declared as a hard dependency by the mod descriptors. DLC-gated content remains conditional on ownership, and this fork has been validated in the maintainer's current DLC environment rather than through a complete no-DLC runtime matrix.

Because this is a total conversion with broad replace paths, combining it with other gameplay, map, country, event or interface overhauls is not recommended unless a dedicated compatibility patch exists.

[h2]Known limitations[/h2]

[list]
[*]Flavor depth is not equal across every country.
[*]Some legacy content still reflects a mod originally built for older Victoria 3 versions.
[*]The technology tree has not yet been fully rebuilt for a 1776 start, so progression can still move too quickly toward later-era technology.
[*]Some historical flags and systems use practical approximations where the game engine requires them.
[*]Future Victoria 3 updates may require additional compatibility work.
[/list]
