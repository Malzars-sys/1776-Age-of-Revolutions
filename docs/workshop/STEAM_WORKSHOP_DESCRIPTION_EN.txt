[h1]1776 - Age of Revolutions[/h1]

[b]Version 2.5.0 Beta[/b]

An unofficial continuation of the original 1776 - Age of Revolutions mod.

Old empires, chartered companies and aristocratic privileges shape the world, while the American Revolutionary War opens a new age of political upheaval. This total conversion brings Victoria 3 back to 1776 and rebuilds its political, technological and economic starting conditions around the period, with room for alternative outcomes.

[h2]The World of 1776[/h2]

[list]
[*]A 1776 start during the American Revolutionary War.
[*]Countries, subjects, borders, governments, laws, institutions and diplomatic relations adapted to the setting.
[*]Historical rulers, military leaders, armies and navies, with country-specific events and Journal Entries.
[*]Featured objectives: Battle for India, Age of Revolutions, Imperial Rivalries, Mercantile Republics and Learn the Game.
[*]Worldwide redistribution of starting buildings, trade centres, government administrations and resource production.
[*]English and French localisation.
[/list]

[h2]Technology, Industry and Infrastructure[/h2]

[list]
[*]Production, Military and Society technology trees reworked for an earlier start, with progression through the 18th and early 19th centuries into later industrial development.
[*]Starting technologies historically reassessed for more than 470 country tags.
[*]16 additional market goods and 10 additional building types.
[*]Expanded production chains for salt, spices, cement, industrial chemicals, petroleum products, copper, aluminium, phosphates, precision machinery and pharmaceuticals.
[*]Research data, scientific management, printing and healthcare integrated into the economy.
[*]Regional Infrastructure covering roads, canals, railways and passenger transport. Improved roads supply Infrastructure and small amounts of Transportation.
[*]AI infrastructure management that favours available technological upgrades and concentrates canals in each country's highest-GDP states.
[/list]

[h2]Imperial Politics[/h2]

The Ottoman Empire faces two distinct challenges, introduced through immersive opening events:

[list]
[*][b]The Burden of the Sublime Porte[/b] — reform administration, taxation and institutions through ten objectives that progressively alleviate the administrative burden. Sustained consolidation is required for lasting success.
[*][b]An Empire of Many Masters[/b] — manage provincial authority, subjects and foreign interference through alternative political paths. Failure can lead into The Sick Man of Europe.
[/list]

The setting also includes Mamluk-led Egypt under Ottoman suzerainty, historical European alliances and trade privileges, and Russian influence over Poland-Lithuania and Crimea through the power-bloc system. Membership does not automatically annex these countries, and Poland is not partitioned merely because a fixed timer expires.

[h2]Armies and Fleets[/h2]

Starting forces and unit availability follow the mod's military progression. Naval options include economical caravels, coastal galleys and transport cogs alongside frigates and ships of the line. Ship-designer equipment offers different hull, armament, propulsion, supply and operational choices. The early classes currently reuse standard wooden-ship 3D models.

[h2]Presentation[/h2]

Dedicated goods, production-method and naval-equipment icons, with consistent colour coding, support the expanded systems. Selected historical monarchs wear powdered wigs; Frederick the Great has a customised portrait with a fitted hat, ribbon, trim and cane. A historical-art frontend, loading screen, objective illustrations and looping title music complete the presentation.

[h2]Beta Status and Compatibility[/h2]

[b]Designed for Victoria 3 1.13 / The Great Wave.[/b] Compatibility with later versions is not claimed. As a total conversion, it is not recommended alongside other major map, technology, country or gameplay overhauls without a compatibility patch.

[b]Start a new campaign to receive the full starting setup.[/b] Existing saves do not automatically receive changes to starting buildings, populations, formations or diplomacy.

The beta remains under development. Economic balance, bureaucracy, employment, market access, resources and visual coverage are still being refined; historical detail varies by country. Some legacy issues and placeholders remain. Feedback and bug reports are welcome. The next regional map rework is not included in this version.
