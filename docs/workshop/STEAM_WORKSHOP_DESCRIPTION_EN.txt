[h1]1776 - Age of Revolutions[/h1]

[b]Version 2.6.0 Beta — Victoria 3 1.15[/b]

An unofficial continuation of the original 1776 - Age of Revolutions mod.

Old empires, chartered companies and aristocratic privileges shape the world, while the American Revolutionary War opens an age of political upheaval. This total conversion brings Victoria 3 back to 1 January 1776 and rebuilds its political, technological and economic starting conditions, with room for alternative history.

[h2]The world of 1776[/h2]
[list]
[*]Countries, borders, governments, laws, institutions and diplomatic relations adapted to the period.
[*]A territorial rework covering the Holy Roman Empire, the Balkans, Scandinavia and Finland, Russia's frontiers, Africa, and several regions of Asia and the Americas.
[*]Kingdoms, principalities, colonial companies and decentralized communities, with different degrees of autonomy.
[*]Historical rulers, revised armies and fleets, national events, and redistributed starting populations, buildings and resources.
[*]Objectives: Battle for India, Age of Revolutions, Imperial Rivalries, Mercantile Republics and Learn the Game. English and French localisation.
[/list]

[h2]Technology and economy[/h2]
[list]
[*]Reworked Production, Military and Society technology trees spanning the 18th century and later industrial development, with starting technologies adapted to national circumstances.
[*]Additional goods and buildings: salt, spices, cement, chemicals, petroleum, copper, aluminium, phosphates, precision machinery and pharmaceuticals. Alloys and hardware create new demand for metals.
[*]Research data, Scientific Management, printing and healthcare integrated into the economy. Companies and prestige goods include the Anzin mines and their prestige coal.
[*]Regional Infrastructure: roads, canals, railways and passenger transport. Improved roads supply Infrastructure and Transportation; industries and agricultural buildings have appropriate transport production methods.
[/list]

[h2]Empires and national situations[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Holy Roman Empire[/b] — estates and electors, elections, a constitution, cooperation, reforms and centralization; obligations, territorial restitution and autonomy.
[*][b]Ottoman Empire[/b] — The Burden of the Sublime Porte calls for administrative, fiscal and institutional reform. An Empire of Many Masters confronts the Porte with provincial powers, subjects and foreign interference; failure can lead into The Sick Man of Europe.
[*][b]Russia[/b] — distinctive administration and state religion, Internal Colonization and Cossack settlements. Russian influence over Poland-Lithuania and Crimea also operates through power blocs.
[*][b]Morocco and Japan[/b] — regional disorder in Morocco and economic and political choices during Japanese subsistence crises.
[/list]

[h2]The American Revolution[/h2]

France may contact the insurgents, after which Congress can request aid and negotiate its commitments. A ten-year convention can provide £30,000 per week, small arms, artillery and instructors in exchange for an obligation and repayable debt, with or without interest. The chain also works with an AI-controlled United States.

French intervention becomes progressively more likely, and France can call Spain through the Family Compact. Minutemen are active from the start; the conflict's outcome opens different military reorganization paths.

[h2]Armies and fleets[/h2]
[list]
[*]Land fortifications in inland states: five levels maximum, three garrison tiers, stronger defense and reduced casualties and territorial losses.
[*]An engineer corps specialized in neutralizing fort protection, with cultural illustrations and detailed numerical tooltips.
[*]Economical caravels, coastal galleys, transport cogs and armed merchantmen alongside frigates and ships of the line.
[*]Ship designer: hull, armament, propulsion, supply and operational choices. Armed merchantmen have their own equipment progression, emphasizing transport and escort duties.
[/list]

The early naval classes currently reuse standard wooden-ship 3D models.

[h2]Presentation[/h2]

Dedicated icons, customised portraits, powdered wigs for selected monarchs, cultural illustrations, historical-art menus, a loading screen and looping title music accompany the conversion.

[h2]Beta and compatibility[/h2]

[b]Adapted to Victoria 3 1.15 / State and Revolution.[/b]

[b]Start a new campaign[/b] to receive the revised starting borders, populations, laws, buildings, armies and diplomatic relations.

Other major map, technology, country or gameplay overhauls require a compatibility patch. The beta remains under development: balance, borders and visuals are still being refined, and content depth varies by country. Feedback and bug reports are welcome.
