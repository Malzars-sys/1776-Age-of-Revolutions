Version 2.4.0 Beta

This is the mod's largest update so far, focused on the technology tree, production, infrastructure, and the historical 1776 economy.

The early Production, Military, and Society technology trees now follow a more specific eighteenth- and early nineteenth-century progression. Naval development has its own historical line within the Military tree. Starting technologies were reassessed across 471 country tags, and later research was reconnected to the new early eras. English and French technology descriptions were also revised.

Sixteen new market goods and ten new building types expand salt, spices and fine food, limestone and cement, industrial chemicals, petroleum products, copper and aluminium, phosphates, precision machinery, and pharmaceuticals. Existing industries have new traditional-to-industrial production paths. The chemical plant now combines fertilizer, chemical, and pharmaceutical methods, while steel mills represent coke-based industrial metallurgy.

Regional Infrastructure replaces the railway-only concept: roads and selected canals serve the 1776 world before rail networks and passenger trains become available. Starting buildings, trade centers, administration, company assets, and many state populations have been revisited worldwide to better represent the period.

This is a beta. Some intended starting buildings may not load because duplicate state-history blocks remain, and some resource placements, market prices, employment, and bureaucracy still need a fresh-game check. A few goods retain placeholder icons. Please report bugs, economic imbalances, historical inconsistencies, and localization problems, ideally with a new save and game log.
