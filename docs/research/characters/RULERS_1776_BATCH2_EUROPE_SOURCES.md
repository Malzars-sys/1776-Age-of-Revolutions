# RULERS_1776_BATCH2_EUROPE_SOURCES

Historical research packet for **1 January 1776**, Batch 2: European dynastic states, Italian republics, the Papal States, Malta, and the Danubian principalities.

## Method

- Target date: **1776-01-01**.
- Institutional, national-biographical, museum, archive, and specialist encyclopaedic sources are preferred.
- Exact `birth_date` is supplied only where the research is sufficiently precise.
- Approximate biographical chronology is deliberately encoded as `age = X` when an exact day/month is not secure.
- Government titles are those appropriate to 1776, not later Napoleonic or nineteenth-century titles.
- Shared monarchies and regencies are explicitly flagged so Codex does not create duplicate rulers merely because Victoria 3 has separate country tags.

## German and Central European states

### BAV — Bavaria — Maximilian III Joseph
- Deutsche Biographie: https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd118579436.html
- Exact birth: 28 March 1727; Elector since 1745; death 30 December 1777.

### SAX — Saxony — Frederick Augustus III
- Deutsche Biographie: https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd119165198.html
- Born 23 December 1750. In 1776 he is **Elector Frederick Augustus III**, not yet King Frederick Augustus I.

### WUR — Württemberg — Karl Eugen
- Deutsche Biographie: https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd118560158.html
- Born 11 February 1728; Duke of Württemberg.

### BAD — Baden — Karl Friedrich
- Deutsche Biographie: https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz53394.html
- Born 22 November 1728. In 1776 title = **Margrave**, not later Elector/Grand Duke.

### BRA — Brunswick-Wolfenbüttel — Karl I
- Deutsche Biographie: https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd10044220X.html
- Born 1 August 1713; reigning Duke.

### HEK — Hesse-Kassel — Friedrich II
- Deutsche Biographie: https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz27764.html
- Born 14 August 1720; Landgrave; personally Catholic from 1749.

### HES — Hesse-Darmstadt — Ludwig IX
- Deutsche Biographie: https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz54750.html
- Born 15 December 1719; Landgrave 1768–1790.

### MEC — Mecklenburg-Schwerin — Friedrich II “the Pious”
- Deutsche Biographie: https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd100619258.html
- Born 9 November 1717; succeeded 30 May 1756.

### MST — Mecklenburg-Strelitz — Adolf Friedrich IV
- House of Mecklenburg-Strelitz: https://mecklenburg-strelitz.org/history/grand-dukes/adolf-friedrich-iv-duke-of-mecklenburg/
- Deutsche Biographie family article: https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd14268130X.html
- Born 5 May 1738; succeeded 11 December 1752; ruled until 1794.

### WEI — Saxe-Weimar-Eisenach — Karl August
- Deutsche Biographie: https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd11856014X.html
- Anna Amalia: https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd118649485.html
- Born 3 September 1757. Anna Amalia’s regency ended in 1775; Karl August is therefore ruler at scenario start.

### MEI — Saxe-Meiningen — Charlotte Amalie + Karl Wilhelm
- Charlotte Amalie, Hessische Biografie/LAGIS: https://www.lagis-hessen.de/de/subjects/rsrec/current/1/entry/sachsen-meiningen%252C%2Bcharlotte%2Bamalie%2Bherzogin%2Bvon/mode/categories/sn/bio/register/person
- Karl Wilhelm, Deutsche Biographie: https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd121892506.html
- Contemporary/historical state lexicon digitised by Freiburg University: https://dl.ub.uni-freiburg.de/diglit/staatslexikon1865-13/0050/ocr
- The sources agree that Charlotte Amalie remained a governing regent into this period; evidence also places Karl Wilhelm in active/co-government from 1775. Treat this as a **regency/co-government special case**, not two ordinary simultaneous rulers.

### COB — Saxe-Coburg-Saalfeld — Ernst Friedrich
- Deutsche Biographie: https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/100265979.html
- Born 8 March 1724; succeeded in 1764.

### LIP — Lippe-Detmold — Simon August
- Deutsche Biographie: https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd189485582.html
- Lippe family article: https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz51757.html
- Born 12 January 1727. Use **Count**, not Prince, for the 1776 title.

### OLD — Oldenburg — Friedrich August
- Deutsche Biographie: https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz69836.html
- Oldenburg family article: https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz73357.html
- Born 20 September 1711. Oldenburg transferred to the younger Holstein-Gottorp line in 1773 and became a duchy in 1774.

## Italy, Venice, Genoa, Papacy and Malta

### SAR — Sardinia-Piedmont — Victor Amadeus III
- Treccani: https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/vittorio-amedeo-iii-re-di-sardegna_%28Enciclopedia-Italiana%29/
- Treccani online: https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/vittorio-amedeo-iii-re-di-sardegna/
- Born 26 June 1726; king from 19 February 1773.

### TUS — Tuscany — Pietro Leopoldo
- Dizionario Biografico degli Italiani / Treccani:
  https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/pietro-leopoldo-d-asburgo-lorena-granduca-di-toscana-poi-imperatore-del-sacro-romano-impero-come-leopoldo-ii_%28Dizionario-Biografico%29/
- Born 3 May 1747; Grand Duke from 1765. Do not use his later imperial title in 1776.

### PAP — Papal States — Pius VI
- Vatican: https://www.vatican.va/content/pius-vi/en.html
- Treccani, Enciclopedia dei Papi: https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/pio-vi_%28Enciclopedia-dei-Papi%29/
- Born 25 December 1717; elected pope in February 1775.

### PAR — Parma — Ferdinand
- Treccani: https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/ferdinando-duca-di-parma-e-piacenza_%28Enciclopedia-Italiana%29/
- Treccani online: https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/ferdinando-di-borbone-duca-di-parma-e-di-piacenza/

### MOD — Modena — Francesco III d’Este
- Treccani: https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/francesco-iii-d-este-duca-di-modena-e-reggio/
- Ercole III cross-check: https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/ercole-iii-d-este-duca-di-modena-e-reggio/
- Francesco III was still duke on 1 January 1776. Ercole III succeeds only in 1780.

### MLT — Order of Malta — Fra’ Emmanuel de Rohan-Polduc
- Sovereign Order of Malta, Grand Masters: https://www.orderofmalta.int/history/the-grand-masters/
- Order of Malta 250th-anniversary notice: https://postemagistrali.orderofmalta.int/en/stamps/250th-anniversary-of-the-election-of-the-grand-master-fra-emmanuel-de-rohan-polduc/
- Institutionally confirmed Grand Master 1775–1797. Exact birth chronology is less central to the institutional sources used here, so packet policy = approximate age 50.

### VEN — Venice — Alvise IV Mocenigo
- Dizionario Biografico / Treccani:
  https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/alvise-mocenigo_res-f8891be5-d924-11e0-8aa7-d5ce3506d72e_%28Dizionario-Biografico%29/
- Born 19 May 1701; elected Doge 19 April 1763; died 31 December 1778.

### GEN — Genoa — Brixio (Brizio/Fabrizio) Giustiniani
- Dizionario Biografico / Treccani:
  https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/brixio-giustiniani_%28Dizionario-Biografico%29/
- Born 28 February 1713; elected Doge 31 January 1775; dogate ends January 1777.
- Therefore he is unambiguously Doge on 1 January 1776.

## Explicitly deferred from this packet

The following tags require a separate constitutional/map audit rather than a quick ruler substitution:

- `SWI` Switzerland — confederation without a single national executive ruler in 1776.
- `ANH` Anhalt — historically fragmented among multiple Anhalt principalities; a single `ANH` tag is an abstraction.
- `NAS` Nassau — historically fragmented; single-tag ruler assignment requires identifying what the fork intends.
- `SER` Serbia — not an independent Serbian state in 1776; this is primarily a map/setup question.
- `LUC` Lucca — oligarchic republic with rapidly rotating chief magistracy; needs exact office-date research and a government abstraction.
- `HAM`, `BRE`, `FRM`, `LUB` — free-city republican governments; research should be done as a dedicated “collective/republican executives” batch.
- `CRI` Crimean Khanate — important but better handled in the next Black Sea/Caucasus/Ottoman-border batch.
