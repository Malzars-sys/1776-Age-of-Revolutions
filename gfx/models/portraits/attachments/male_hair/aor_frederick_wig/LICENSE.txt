Adapted for 1776 Age of Revolutions: positioned for the Victoria 3 male head,
skinned to its portrait rig, converted to PDX mesh, and texture channels packed
for native portrait materials with 1024-pixel DDS textures and internal mipmaps.
The inner shell is adapted as an opaque wig body; the outer fibre shell retains
cutout opacity with coverage-preserving mipmaps.
The powdered colour and roughness are calibrated for native portrait lighting;
packed normal/material channels are filtered independently in mipmaps.
