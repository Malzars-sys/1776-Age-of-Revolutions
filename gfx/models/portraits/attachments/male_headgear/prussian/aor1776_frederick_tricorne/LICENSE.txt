Changes: isolated the hat from the welded scanned bust at its anatomical seam;
cleaned geometry/normals; fitted it to the Victoria 3 male head and approved wig;
reduced topology; baked original geometric detail into a new normal atlas;
added a thin open-bottom lining; recoloured as matte charcoal felt; added subtle
felt grain; rigidly attached it to the native bn_h_skull bone; exported PDX mesh
and native DDS material channels/mipmaps. Original scan texture is not used in
the runtime tricorne. No endorsement by the original author is implied.
