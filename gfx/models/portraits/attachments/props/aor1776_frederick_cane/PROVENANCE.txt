Original cane geometry and textures created for this mod from the historical
painting supplied by its maintainer. Dark wooden shaft, light curved handle,
small aged-metal collar and ferrule. Rebuilt by tools/blender/build_frederick_cane.py.

No BlenderKit asset, texture or code is incorporated. In particular,
"Walking Stick" by Alex Sandr (asset_base_id
6c602b85-804a-4d9c-8868-abe7bcc64f5f) is NOT included. Its Royalty Free licence
requires non-extractible distribution; the maintainer instead approved an
original reconstruction for the openly distributed Victoria 3 mod.

Installed Victoria 3 assets serve solely as local technical references for
scale, coordinate convention, bn_r_prop attachment and the cane-holding pose.
No vanilla geometry, texture or animation file is redistributed with this cane.
